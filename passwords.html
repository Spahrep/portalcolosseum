Password Guidance

Your password is your account. We don't enforce character classes (no uppercase + symbol + number mandates) — just a minimum of 8 characters, or 12+ if you want extra strength. Secure it however makes sense for you.

Need ideas?

  • A memorable sentence with spaces (if our system allows them) — length defeats cracking
  • A diceware passphrase (e.g. correct horse battery staple — pick 6+ random words)
  • Whatever you can remember and don't reuse on every other site
