## Variant: Cost Previews

### Design stance
A dashboard-style card layout that shows AP/Gold costs right on the Portal card so players can decide at a glance without tapping through.

### Key choices
- **Portal card:** Grid-based card showing name, icon, subtitle, AND cost breakdowns (3 AP + 100 gc). The primary action communicates its price before the tap.
- **Standard cards:** Icon + label + sub-description + chevron. Each card includes a one-liner so the player knows what's inside.
- **AP bar:** Visual meter instead of just text — gives a quick read of remaining resource.
- **HUD:** Slightly taller than variant A, with an actual progress bar for AP.
- **Logout:** Demoted to gray footer text — pure friction.
- **Scrolling:** The card area scrolls if content grows (future-proof for more town options).

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at:** Information density, player knows Portal costs without an extra screen, AP bar gives resource awareness at a glance.
- **Weak at:** More visual noise than Big Chonkers. The cost grid adds complexity to the primary action button.

### Best for
Players who care about cost management — regular runners who always check their AP/Gold before entering a portal and don't want to navigate blind.
