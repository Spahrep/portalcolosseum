# Core Philosophy

## Guiding Principles

- **Action Points (AP) per day**: Limited daily resource that gates all meaningful player actions.
- **Push your luck mechanisms are key**: Core gameplay loop rewards risk assessment and bold decisions.
- **Procedural generation where possible**: Weapons, encounters, and outcomes favor generation over pure selection.
- **Randomness at all stages**: No deterministic outcomes. Examples:
  - Damage is never fixed (e.g., 100 ± X)
  - Loot values, monster HP, and other stats include variance
  - Calibration of variance ranges is critical for fun and fairness

## Inventory Philosophy

Limited carrying capacity forces meaningful choices:
- Initially: 2 hands + 1-2 item slots
- Items support consumables, weapon swaps, or utility
- Possible configurations under consideration: 1 consumable + 1 belt-loop swap weapon

## Scoping Note

MVP focuses on a tight, replayable loop. Advanced systems (gathering, crafting, enchanting, auction house) are tracked in the Idea Bank for post-MVP seasons.
