# AP Economy

## Primary Uses of Action Points

Action Points are the daily limited resource. Current planned expenditures:

1. **Starting a portal run** — the main sink
2. **Refreshing the store** — stores offer 3 items that can be refreshed daily by spending AP

## Store Mechanics (MVP)

- Each store presents 3 items
- Refresh costs AP
- Items are available for purchase with gold

## Gold Uses (Current & Potential)

- Purchasing items from the store
- Potential future uses (MVP scope):
  - Unlocking portals (e.g., beat X-1 + gold cost, or X-2 + higher gold cost)
  - Minor weapon adjustments at a smith (gold ± AP cost)

## Design Constraints

- Must prevent both:
  - Farming of easy portals for unlimited resources
  - Situations where players become soft-locked with no viable portals
- The 3-active-portals limit works in concert with AP economy to maintain healthy pacing

## Future Systems

Additional AP sinks (crafting, enchanting, harvesting) are planned for post-MVP seasons and tracked in the Idea Bank.
