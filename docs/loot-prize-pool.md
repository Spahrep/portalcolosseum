# Loot & Prize Pool

## Prize Pool Mechanics

- After every combat in a run, loot is added to a shared **prize pool**
- **Finish the run**: Player receives the full prize pool
- **Stop early**: Player receives a reduced share of the pool
- **Die during run**: Player receives almost nothing or nothing

This creates strong "push your luck" tension.

## Loot Sources & Scaling

Loot originates from individual combats. Scaling considerations:
- May increase with successive fights in the same run
- May be influenced by the portal's overall difficulty level
- Exact formula will combine fight number and portal tier

## Leaderboards & Recognition

Global leaderboards track the deepest progress:
- Format example: "#1 Bob – Portal 5, fight 4"
- **Seasons** reset competitive rankings periodically while preserving historical records
- **Badges** provide permanent account achievements (e.g., "Portal 3 Slayer", "Completed run with all red dice")
- Potential display of best-run gear and defeated monsters on leaderboard entries

## Design Philosophy

The prize pool + continue/stop choice is central to the push-your-luck identity of Portal Colosseum. Risk/reward decisions happen at every combat boundary.
